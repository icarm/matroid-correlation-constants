Verification language

Exact claims stay separate from lower bounds.

The site records the unweighted invariant \(\overline{\alpha}(M)=\max_{i\ne j} |B^{ij}(M)|\,|B_{ij}(M)|/ (|B^i_j(M)|\,|B^j_i(M)|)\). See the definitions, family proofs, and representability argument on the mathematics page.

Solid curve

Exact family value

The proof identifies an attaining pair and shows that no other eligible pair has a larger ratio.

Dashed curve

Certified lower bound

A distinguished pair is evaluated exactly, but it has not yet been proved to maximize the full invariant.

Catalog point

Automatic exact check

The finite matroid is reconstructed and every eligible pair is enumerated by trusted verifier code.
